# Final Project Scenario: Build Pulse Intelligence

## The scenario

You're the Senior Product Manager at **PulseHQ**. Your team has been asked to help Customer Success teams move faster and stay ahead of customer risk, but there's no room to add headcount. AI presents an opportunity to help the team do more with the information and resources it already has.

Customer information is scattered across CRM records, product usage, support tickets, customer conversations, and renewal data. Customer Success Managers (CSMs) spend too much time piecing together these signals, identifying which accounts need attention, and deciding what to do next. By the time a risk becomes obvious, it may already be too late to prevent churn.

You decide to build **Pulse Intelligence**, an AI-powered customer intelligence platform that helps Customer Success teams turn fragmented customer data into actionable insight.

Three jobs stand out: connecting scattered signals to reveal emerging customer risks, identifying which accounts need attention most, and recommending the next best action to protect customer relationships and retention.

That's what you're building Pulse Intelligence to do.

## What you're building

1. **Detect customer risk.** Analyse customer signals across usage, support, sentiment, and renewals to identify accounts that may need attention.
2. **Explain what's happening.** Bring together relevant evidence so CSMs can understand why an account has been flagged, rather than relying on an unexplained risk score.
3. **Recommend what to do next.** Suggest appropriate next steps based on the evidence, with human review and approval where needed.

The product follows a simple principle:

**Signals → Insights → Risk → Recommendation → Action**

_From fragmented customer data to informed action._

## The challenge

Your job is not simply to add a chatbot or put an AI-generated summary on a dashboard.

You must determine where AI can create genuine customer and business value, how the system should access and interpret information, which tasks it can perform, and where human judgement must remain in control.

You must also establish how to evaluate the quality of its outputs, manage its risks, and take the product from an early prototype to a credible, production-ready proposition.

You will build and test the product progressively throughout the course, using each module's outputs to inform the next.

## What the whole project delivers

By the end of the project, you should be able to demonstrate how you would take an AI product from opportunity identification to a production-ready proposition.

You will have made and documented the key product decisions, built a working prototype, designed its data and retrieval approach, defined human oversight, tested its behaviour against realistic cases, and planned how it could be launched and improved over time.

The finished project should tell one connected story: why Pulse Intelligence needs to exist, what it does, how it works, how you know it is trustworthy, and how it creates measurable value for PulseHQ and its customers.

## The central question

> How can we use AI to help Customer Success teams identify customer risk earlier, understand the evidence, and take the right action before it becomes churn?
